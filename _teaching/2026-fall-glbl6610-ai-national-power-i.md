GLBL 6610. Graduate teaching fellow for Professor Ted Wittenstein.

The first term of a two-term seminar, featuring guest scholars and practitioners from
across the university, on how artificial intelligence may alter the basic building
blocks of world order. After an introduction to the technology and its open research
problems, the seminar works through seven areas where AI and adjacent technologies
already raise security concerns: autonomous weapons and the character of conflict,
disinformation and democratic resilience, U.S.-China competition, AI ethics and
safety, AI governance, nanotechnology and quantum computing, and outer space. The
aim is to give students enough technical fluency to work across the law, technology,
and policy communities rather than within one of them.
