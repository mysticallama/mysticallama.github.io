GLBL 3830. Graduate teaching fellow for Professor Paul Simons.

A seminar on the challenges facing advanced and developing economies as they pursue
the goals of the Paris agreement while maintaining energy security and economic
competitiveness. Students work through the architecture of global energy and climate
governance, the constraints policymakers actually face, and the deployment prospects
for the major clean energy technologies over the coming decades.
