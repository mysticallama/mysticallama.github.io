GLBL 2392. Graduate teaching fellow for Professor Ted Wittenstein.

The course covers the theory and practice of intelligence and its relationship to
American foreign policy and national security decision-making, including the analytic
tools available for assessing international affairs and the problem of communicating
that analysis to senior policymakers. Case studies run from the Second World War to
the present and cover failures as closely as successes.
