<h2 id="about">About</h2>

<div class="about" markdown="1">

I'm Kevin, a Master in Public Policy candidate at the [Yale Jackson School of Global Affairs](https://jackson.yale.edu/), where I'm affiliated with the Schmidt Program on Artificial Intelligence, Emerging Technologies, and National Power. I'm also a Policy Fellow at the Yale Digital Ethics Center and Editor-in-Chief of the [*Yale Journal of International Affairs*](https://www.yalejournal.org/).

My research is on epistemic risk. Governments are starting to lean on AI to draft, summarize and analyze, and that reliance is becoming structural before anyone has worked out how to check it. I want to understand what this does to how states know things, and why the AI safety and international relations communities have had so little to say to each other about it. On the technical side I study latent biases in frontier models and how those models can shape the decisions of the people who use them.

Before graduate school I spent several years as a data scientist and machine learning engineer in the U.S. intelligence community. I studied Quantitative Social Science at Dartmouth.

</div>

<h3 id="now">What I'm working on</h3>

- As an AI Safety Policy Entrepreneurship Fellow at the [Federation of American Scientists](https://fas.org/), I'm building an open protocol for evaluating geopolitical bias in frontier models used for government analysis.
- I'm a graduate research assistant to Gordon Goldstein at the [Council on Foreign Relations](https://www.cfr.org/), working on recursive self-improvement and international security.
- At Augur, I research how AI can support negotiation and national security decision-making.
- My work from this summer's fellowship at the [Centre for the Governance of AI](https://www.governance.ai/) continues: an audit of whether frontier models steer multi-turn negotiations toward particular states' interests.
- With [Doublethink Lab](https://doublethinklab.org/), I study foreign information manipulation and interference around Taiwan.
- With the California Governor's Office of Emergency Services, I'm translating the state's SB 53 frontier AI framework into public-sector crisis management protocols.
- This fall I'm co-mentoring a [SPAR](https://sparai.org/) project on Taiwan's position under transformative AI with David Sánchez García.
